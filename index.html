Our Story

Marigold Cup began with one old espresso machine, a pot of marigolds on the windowsill and a simple wish: to give Lantern Lane a place where everyone feels at home. What started as a tiny corner counter serving coffee to sleepy neighbours has grown into a cosy little café full of chatter, fresh bakes and familiar faces. We still know most of our regulars by name (and by order!), and every cup is made the way we’d make it for a friend.

— From all of us at Marigold Cup
